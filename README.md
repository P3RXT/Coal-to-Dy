Ever found yourself stuck in a world where black dye is simply impossible to obtain?

No oceans. No squids. No Wither roses.
Just you, your world and a missing piece of progression.

**Coal to Dye** is a small but essential datapack that restores balance where it’s often broken.
It introduces a simple, intuitive crafting recipe that allows you to turn coal into black dye.

Perfect for:
- SkyBlock and OneBlock worlds
- Superflat survival
- Challenge maps with limited resources
- Custom datapacks that alter mob spawning
- Players who value consistency and smart design
